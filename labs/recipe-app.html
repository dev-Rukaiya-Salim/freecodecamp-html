Pilau Recipe

Pilau is a special swahili dish prepared mostly on special occassions especially during celebrations.

Ingredients

  • Rice 1kg
  • Masala
  • Meat 1/2kg
  • 3 Onions

Instructions

  1. Cut the onions then put it in the sufuria then add cooking oil
  2. Once the onions turn brown add the meat
  3. Add the masala followed by salt then pour hot water
  4. After 10minutes add the rice then cover until the water dries up
